## Push Up Hold

An isometric push-up that builds chest, shoulder and core strength by holding the lowered position.

**How to**
Start in a push-up position with your hands under your shoulders.
Lower halfway down until your elbows are bent.
Hold there with your body in a straight line.
Breathe steadily and keep your core tight.

**Correct**
Keep your body straight from head to heels.
Keep your core engaged.
Keep your elbows close to your body.
Breathe steadily.

**Mistakes**
Don't let your hips drop.
Don't raise your hips too high.
Don't flare your elbows out wide.
Don't hold your breath.

## Squat Jacks

A cardio squat that mixes a jumping jack with a squat to work the legs, glutes and heart.

**How to**
Stand with your feet together and your hands in front.
Jump your feet out and drop into a squat.
Jump your feet back together as you stand up.
Repeat with a steady rhythm.

**Correct**
Land softly.
Keep your knees over your toes.
Keep your chest up.
Keep your heels down in the squat.

**Mistakes**
Don't land hard.
Don't let your knees cave inward.
Don't round your back.
Don't rush and lose control.

**Source**
Video: How to Do: SQUAT JACKS
URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tcgvAxhEhvQ

## Crescent Low Lunge With Cactus Arms

A low lunge that opens the hips and chest while the bent arms draw the shoulders back.

**How to**
Step one foot forward into a low lunge.
Lower your back knee toward the floor.
Lift your chest and bend your arms into a cactus shape.
Hold, then switch sides.

**Correct**
Keep your front knee over your ankle.
Keep your hips facing forward.
Keep your chest lifted.
Breathe slowly.

**Mistakes**
Don't push your front knee past your toes.
Don't let your lower back arch too much.
Don't shrug your shoulders.
Don't hold your breath.

## Dumbbell Reverse Curl

A biceps and forearm exercise that curls the weights with the palms facing down.

**How to**
Stand tall with a dumbbell in each hand, palms facing down.
Keep your elbows close to your sides.
Curl the weights up toward your shoulders.
Lower them slowly with control.

**Correct**
Keep your elbows still.
Keep your wrists straight.
Move with control.
Keep your back straight.

**Mistakes**
Don't swing the weights.
Don't use your back to lift.
Don't drop the weights down fast.
Don't shrug your shoulders.
