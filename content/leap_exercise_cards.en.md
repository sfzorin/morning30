# Arms & Shoulders

## Standing Forward Bend with Shoulder Opener

A standing forward fold with hands clasped behind the back that stretches the hamstrings and opens the shoulders.

**How to**
Stand with your feet hip-width apart.
Clasp your hands behind your lower back.
Hinge forward from your hips and let your arms rise overhead.
Hold where you feel a stretch in your hamstrings and chest.
Breathe slowly and hold the position.

**Correct**
Keep your back straight as you hinge forward.
Keep a soft bend in your knees.
Breathe slowly throughout the hold.
Let gravity open your shoulders gradually.

**Mistakes**
Don't round your back on the way down.
Don't lock your knees.
Don't force the stretch.
Don't hold your breath.

**Source**

## Seated Overhead Tricep Extension

A seated overhead tricep extension that isolates the triceps by extending a dumbbell from behind the head.

**How to**

Raise the dumbbell overhead and lower it behind your head by bending your elbows.
Extend your arms back to the top, squeezing your triceps.
Keep your elbows pointing forward throughout.
Repeat for the desired reps.

**Correct**
Keep your elbows close to your head.
Keep your core braced and your back upright.

Lower with control.

**Mistakes**
Don't let your elbows flare out wide.
Don't arch your lower back.
Don't use momentum to swing the weight up.

## Star Jumps

An explosive full-body jump that raises the heart rate and works the legs, glutes and shoulders.

**How to**

Bend your knees slightly, then jump and spread your legs wide.

Keep your core braced on landing.
Keep your arms controlled.

**Mistakes**
Don't land hard.
Don't let your knees cave inward.
Don't lock your knees on landing.
Don't let your torso lean too far forward.

Breathe rhythmically.

**Mistakes**

Don't collapse your shoulders.
Don't hold your breath.
